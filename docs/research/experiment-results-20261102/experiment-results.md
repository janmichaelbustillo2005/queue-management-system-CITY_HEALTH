# WPS-A versus WRR: Regular-majority and Vulnerable-majority experiments

> SYNTHETIC DATA ONLY. Every workload parameter is an assumption (backend/simulation/experiment-scenarios.js),
> not a City Health Office measurement. Both algorithms serve the identical workload for each seed, in the
> same shared queue with three general doctors; only the choice of the next patient differs.

Generated by `node simulation/experiment-report.js` — 30 seeds per experiment (20261029–20261058).
Values are mean ± 95% CI half-width across seeds; the last column counts the seeds in which each algorithm was better (as displayed; capacity measures within 1% are ties).

## Workload assumptions

- Vulnerable mix (share among Vulnerable patients, apportioned exactly): senior 34%, pwd 14%, pregnant 16%, indigenous 8%, solo_parent 8%, senior+pwd 10%, pregnant+solo_parent 5%, pregnant+indigenous 3%, pwd+solo_parent 2%.
- Stress mix: senior 45%, pwd 20%, pregnant 20%, senior+pwd 15% among Vulnerable; 15% Regular.
- Scenario A/B registrations per hour from 08:00 (relative weights): 16, 15, 13, 10, 7, 8, 6, 5.
- Starvation evidence: still waiting at the last registration, after 60 minutes in which every call (at least 3) went to a later registration.

## Evaluation criteria and relative importance

- **Primary: Priority-lane protection of Vulnerable patients.** The office's priority policy puts Senior, PWD and Pregnant patients ahead of Regular patients; a call that breaks it is visible to patients and staff.
- **Primary: Bounded waiting for Regular patients (no starvation).** A Regular patient must eventually be seen even on a day with many Vulnerable registrations.
- **Secondary: Average waiting time.** Matters for overall satisfaction, but averages can hide a few very long waits.
- **Context: Throughput and doctor utilization.** Both algorithms keep every free doctor busy whenever someone waits, so these are expected to match; a difference only reflects which consultation lengths ended last.

No overall winner is declared: the two Primary criteria are in tension, and the choice depends on the weight the City Health Office gives to each.
